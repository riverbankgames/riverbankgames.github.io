Privacy policy

Beaver Dam Jam: Brain Puzzles (Android) · Last updated 30 September 2026

Who we are

Beaver Dam Jam: Brain Puzzles ("the game") is made by Riverbank Games, run by Vlad Magiar, Timișoara, Romania. We are responsible for the personal data the game handles. For any privacy question or request, write to riverbank.games.app@gmail.com.

In short

  • You don't need an account. The game never asks for your name, email, contacts or photos.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch a video for a reward. To show and measure ads, Google collects some information about your device.
  • We use Google's analytics and crash reports to see which levels are too hard and to fix crashes.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy options.
  • We don't sell your data.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps a small save file on your phone: your level, coins, boosters, album, hearts and settings. It never leaves your phone except through Android backup, below.

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies this save file to your own Google account so your progress comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Google runs the backup under your account; we can't see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

A video ad plays only when you tap to watch one, for example to open an extra pier. The Google Mobile Ads software in the game collects:

  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location (country or city, never your precise location);
  • how you interact with the app and with ads (app launches, taps, video views);
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ads software perform;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), ads are personalised only if you agree in the consent message; otherwise ads are limited or not shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads in the game are limited to content rated PG or below (suitable for most audiences). Google acts as an independent controller for ads; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Analytics. Google Analytics for Firebase records how the game is played: sessions, screens, and game events such as a level started, won or lost, a booster used or a video watched. With them it records a random ID for the installation (the app instance ID), your device model and Android version, and an approximate location derived from a shortened IP address. Where consent is required, analytics storage follows your answer in the consent message. We see only totals, such as how many players finish level 12, never who you are.

Crash reports. If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: where in the code it happened, the state of the game at that moment, your device model and Android version, and a random ID for the installation. We use it only to fix crashes.

Google processes this data for us under the Firebase data processing terms.

Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games, the game receives your Play Games player ID and gamer name from Google and saves your progress in your Play Games account, so you can continue on another phone. Google stores these saved games; you can see and delete your Play Games data in your Play Games profile settings.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. If we add them, payments go through Google Play, we receive only a confirmation of the order, and we will update this policy first.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed, write to us.

Who receives data

We don't run our own servers. The data above goes to Google (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play Games), which may process it outside the EEA under standard contractual clauses. We don't sell or rent personal data, and we share it with no one else. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long data is kept

  • The save file stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Analytics data linked to an installation ID is deleted after 2 months (Google Analytics' standard setting).
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Ad data is kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Emails to us are kept only as long as needed to answer them, and at most a year.

Security

Everything the game sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our Google developer accounts is protected with two-step verification.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw your consent any time: in the game, Settings → Privacy options (shown in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the save file from your phone.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy. Because the game has no accounts, we can't look you up by name; email us and we'll help with what we can identify.
  • You can complain to a data protection authority. In Romania it is the ANSPDCP.

Legal bases: your consent for personalised ads and, where asked, analytics storage; our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and fair (crash reports, fraud prevention, non-personalised ads and game statistics).

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
